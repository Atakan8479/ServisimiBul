### Dated instruments found for this record

- **VERBIS exemption, current text — fetched directly from kvkk.gov.tr.** KVKK Board Decision **2018/87** set the original general exemption: fewer than 50 annual employees and an annual balance-sheet total under 100 million TL, for controllers whose main activity is not special-category data processing. Board Decision **2025/1572** (dated **04.09.2025**, Official Gazette **01.10.2025**) added a separate exemption for controllers whose main activity **is** special-category data processing, with fewer than 10 annual employees and an annual balance-sheet total under 10 million TL. Board Decision **2025/2393** (dated **25.12.2025**) clarified how the two thresholds combine. The Authority's own public announcement, **[Kişisel Verileri Koruma Kurulunun 04.09.2025 tarihli ve 2025/1572 sayılı Kararının Uygulama Esaslarına İlişkin Kamuoyu Duyurusu](https://www.kvkk.gov.tr/Icerik/8577/kisisel-verileri-koruma-kurulunun-04-09-2025-tarihli-ve-2025-1572-sayili-kararinin-uygulama-esaslarina-iliskin-kamuoyu-duyurusu)**, published **12.01.2026**, was fetched directly for this record and confirms: it cites both 2025/1572 and 2025/2393; for controllers keeping balance-sheet books, annual employee count and annual balance-sheet total apply **cumulatively**; for controllers not keeping balance-sheet books, only the employee-count criterion applies (no balance-sheet total exists for them). **Source level: direct**, this announcement page itself, for the rule described above. The underlying Board decision texts (2025/1572, 2025/2393) were not separately fetched from the Kurul Kararları index; counsel should confirm the announcement's restatement against those decisions before relying on it.
- **ETBIS — fetched directly from ticaret.gov.tr.** [ticaret.gov.tr/ic-ticaret/elektronik-ticaret/mevzuat](https://ticaret.gov.tr/ic-ticaret/elektronik-ticaret/mevzuat) lists Law No. 6563 (Resmî Gazete 05.11.2014, amended by Law No. 7416 of 07.07.2022) and distinguishes two separate instruments that this record's earlier draft conflated: (1) the **"Elektronik Ticaret Bilgi Sistemi ve Bildirim Yükümlülükleri Hakkında Tebliğ"** (notification communiqué, in force **11.08.2017**), which sets the ETBİS **registration and notification** obligation itself for service providers and intermediary service providers, and (2) the **"Elektronik Ticaret Aracı Hizmet Sağlayıcı ve Elektronik Ticaret Hizmet Sağlayıcılar Hakkında Yönetmelik"** (Resmî Gazete **29.12.2022, No. 32058**), which governs substantive operating rules (buyer-information duties, intermediary-contract content, IP-infringement prevention, advertising limits, licensing) rather than the registration trigger. The 2022 regulation replaced an earlier, since-repealed 2015 regulation of the same subject area (Resmî Gazete 26.08.2015, No. 29457). **Source level: direct**, this Ministry page, for the instrument titles and dates above.
- **İYS — fetched directly from ticaret.gov.tr.** The same Ministry page confirms the governing text is the **"Ticari İletişim ve Ticari Elektronik İletiler Hakkında Yönetmelik,"** amended **04.01.2020**. This record's earlier draft cited a 04.01.2022 amendment date; that date is not supported by the Ministry's own page and is corrected here to **04.01.2020**. The Ministry's İYS page ([ticaret.gov.tr/ic-ticaret/bilgi-sistemleri/ticari-elektronik-ileti-yonetim-sistemi-iys](https://ticaret.gov.tr/ic-ticaret/bilgi-sistemleri/ticari-elektronik-ileti-yonetim-sistemi-iys)) was also fetched directly for this record; it names no further Resmî Gazete number or date for the registration obligation itself, only April 2023 as its own article-publication date. A 2020 extension of the original registration deadline (secondary sources differ on 01.12.2020 vs. 16.01.2021) is a historical implementation date, not a current obligation, and is not relied on here. **Source level: direct** for the regulation title and the 04.01.2020 amendment date; the deadline-extension detail remains secondary and unused.

## 3. Applicability criteria and what they require from the business

Each regime's registration obligation turns on business facts that are either not yet decided (see LEG-01, section 1) or not recorded anywhere in this repository. This record does not estimate or assume any of them.

### T1 — VERBIS

- **Criteria that drive the obligation:** the data controller's legal form (natural person vs. legal entity), annual employee count, annual balance sheet total, whether personal-data processing is the controller's main activity, and whether special-category data or data of other controllers is processed on their behalf — thresholds and exemption categories are set by KVKK Board decisions, not estimated here.
- **Missing business metrics (not in this repository):** identity and legal form of the data controller (LEG-01 L1, `[PENDING]`), employee count, annual balance sheet/turnover. None of these exist in the codebase; they are organizational facts only the owner can supply.
- **Verified product facts bearing on it:** the platform processes personal data of parents, operators, and site visitors (LEG-02 inventory, `docs/19_PERSONAL_DATA_PROCESSING_MAP.md`); no special-category data is collected by design (D015; LEG-02 section 5; LEG-08 section 2.4).
- **Dependency:** blocked on LEG-01 L1 (controller identity/legal form) and on owner-supplied employee/turnover figures that do not exist in this repository.

### T2 — ETBIS

- **Criteria that drive the obligation, per the Ministry's own regulation (section 2):** registration attaches to **conducting electronic commerce activity** — a service provider offering goods/services in its own electronic commerce environment, or an intermediary service provider (aracı hizmet sağlayıcı) enabling others' electronic commerce, each before starting that activity. **Charging revenue, commission, or a fee is not stated as the trigger for registration; a free or non-monetized electronic-commerce or intermediary activity can still fall within scope.** This record corrects an earlier draft that implied revenue/commission was itself the mandatory condition; it is not, under the regulation's own wording, and must not be presented as one in any future text. The actual, unresolved question for counsel is narrower: whether the platform's current functionality (discovery, demand coordination, public comments; no ordering, booking, or transaction completion — section 3 of LEG-01) amounts to "elektronik ticaret" or intermediary activity under the regulation at all, independent of whether money changes hands.
- **Verified product facts:** no feature to book, reserve, order, or pay exists, and no payment code exists in the repository (LEG-01 section 2; confirmed again by this record: no `payment`/`Payment`/`invoice`/`Invoice` symbol anywhere in `servisimibul-backend/src/main/java`). No commission, listing fee, or featured placement is implemented. These facts describe current functionality; they are evidence for counsel's opinion, not a substitute for it, and their absence does not by itself establish non-applicability.
- **Missing business metrics:** whether any future commission, fee, or paid-placement model is planned, and when (LEG-01 L6, `[PENDING]`); the ETBIS applicability table's own trigger list in LEG-01 section 4 lists the same reassessment triggers. These bear on L6/L7, not on whether registration itself is revenue-conditioned.
- **Dependency:** blocked on LEG-01 L3 and L7 (counsel's opinion on the platform's legal role and on ETBİS applicability specifically, applying the Ministry regulation's actual criteria to section 2's facts).

### T3 — İYS

- **Criteria that drive the obligation:** sending commercial electronic messages (marketing email, SMS, or calls) to recipients; the registration and consent-management duty attaches to the sender of such messages, not to transactional/service messages.
- **Verified product facts:** the repository contains SMTP **configuration support**, not enabled email delivery. `servisimibul.email.enabled` defaults to `false` in `application.properties` (`${SB_MAIL_ENABLED:false}`), and `AuthEmailSender.java` wires `DisabledAuthEmailSender` whenever that property is `false` or absent (`@ConditionalOnProperty(..., havingValue = "false", matchIfMissing = true)`); `SmtpAuthEmailSender` only activates if `servisimibul.email.enabled=true` is explicitly set. `application-prod.properties` reads `SB_MAIL_ENABLED` from the environment with no default, so production delivery requires that variable and SMTP credentials to be set outside the repository; nothing in the repository sets them. No value for `SB_MAIL_ENABLED` is recorded anywhere in this repository, so this record cannot and does not claim whether email delivery is active in any deployed environment — only that the code ships disabled by default and that, even if enabled, the only wired message types are account/email verification, password recovery, and email-change verification (`AuthEmailSender` call sites in `AuthService.java`: `sendVerification`, `sendPasswordReset`, `sendEmailChangeVerification`), none of them marketing content. The email-change path has its own separate gate, `app.auth.email-change.enabled=false`, so it stays disabled in production regardless of `SB_MAIL_ENABLED`. No marketing-email or SMS code path exists in the repository. No analytics, advertising, or tracking script is loaded (`servisimibul-frontend/index.html`, `package.json` — reconfirmed by this record, no hits for `analytics`/`gtag`/`cookie`). These facts refine, and do not contradict, LEG-01 section 2.
- **Technical input now exists; legal decisions remain missing:** LEG-09 (`docs/30_COOKIE_TRACKING_AND_MARKETING_CONSENT.md`) verifies one first-party server-session cookie, no analytics/advertising/third-party browser tracker, no marketing email/SMS/call path, and account/security-only SMTP call sites. Its pending C4 will classify those account/security messages and provide the supporting legal reasoning and facts to this record. C4 does not decide İYS applicability.
- **Dependency:** the technical inventory is satisfied. T3 remains exclusively a LEG-10 decision and is blocked until C4 supplies the message-classification input and counsel applies it with LEG-01 L7; absence of a marketing feature is evidence, not an approved non-applicability conclusion.

1. **Owner evidence first.** The owner supplies the controller's legal form (or confirms it is still undetermined, see LEG-01 L1), employee count, annual balance sheet/turnover, and any revenue-model statement (LEG-01 L6).
2. **Review LEG-09.** The owner decides its proposed product rules and qualified Turkish counsel answers C1, C3, and C4; C4 records the message classification and inputs for T3, not T3 itself. Merging the technical inventory alone is not completion or approval.
3. **Counsel review.** Qualified Turkish counsel applies section 3's criteria to the supplied facts and the reviewed LEG-09 C4 input, then records the separate LEG-10 opinions for T1–T3.
4. **Record the answers.** The owner fills section 1 with the reviewed outcome, owner, reviewer, evidence reference (no privileged content), and date, and adds an approved `D0xx` entry to `docs/01_DECISIONS.md`.
5. **Release gate.** Any registration action, or any conclusion that registration is unnecessary, is reviewed against the LEG-08–LEG-11 launch-gate line in `docs/25_LEGAL_APPROVAL_TRACKER.md` section 5 before a real-user pilot or public launch.
